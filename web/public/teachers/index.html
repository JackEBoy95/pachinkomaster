Why It Works in Classrooms

Most random name pickers produce a result instantly and play a short animation to cover the gap. Students figure this out quickly — the outcome is predetermined, the spin is just theatre. Once they know that, the tool loses its credibility.

PachinkoMaster uses real physics simulation. Each ball follows a genuinely unpredictable path through a board of pegs, and nobody knows where it will land until it does. Students watch their coloured ball bouncing in real time. That visible randomness builds trust in a way no spinner can match — because there's nothing to doubt. They saw it happen.

The secondary benefit is engagement. Five seconds of watching a ball ricochet through a board keeps an entire class focused in a way that a button click never will.

Quick Setup

  1. Open pachinkomaster.com in your browser. No account needed.
  2. Add your students in the Players panel. Type names or paste a list.
  3. Set up prize slots for participation, categories, or tournament scoring.
  4. Bookmark the URL. Your class list saves automatically — reopen it next lesson and everything is there.
  5. Project it on your classroom display and drop.
