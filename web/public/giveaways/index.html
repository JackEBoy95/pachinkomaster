The Giveaway Tool Your Audience Will Trust (and Love)

Most giveaway tools pick a winner behind the scenes and play animation to cover it up. PachinkoMaster is different: the randomness happens live on screen. Your audience watches every ball bounce through a physics board in real time — no hidden algorithm, no reason to doubt the result. Just physics.

Why Physics Beats a Button

When you press a button and a winner appears, your audience has to take your word for it. The result is invisible — generated on a server they can't see by code they can't inspect. For most giveaways that's fine. But once chat starts questioning a result, that trust is hard to rebuild.

A physics-based ball drop is verifiable by anyone watching. The ball starts at the top and falls through a board of pegs under real simulated physics. Nobody knows where it will land, including the software, until it gets there. That genuine uncertainty is visible — and visible randomness creates trust in a way hidden algorithms never can.

The second benefit is entertainment. Five seconds of watching a ball ricochet toward a winner creates actual suspense. Chat reacts to each collision. People track their name. That engagement is the kind of moment that gets clipped.

Setup in 60 Seconds

  1. Collect your entries. Grab the list of eligible viewer names from your giveaway — followers, subs, command-responders, whatever your criteria is.
  2. Paste names into PachinkoMaster. Open the Players panel and add participants. No account needed.
  3. Set up prize slots. Create a "Winner" slot (narrow for drama) and fill the rest with "No Win." Add more Winner slots for multi-prize giveaways.
  4. Enable Theatre Mode for a hands-free automated drop during your stream.
  5. Hit Drop All and watch with your chat.

Multi-Winner Giveaways

If you have five prizes, drop all five balls at once. They land in different slots, and the order gives you a natural first-through-fifth ranking for prize selection. No re-rolling, no spin-again awkwardness. Name the prize slots "Grand Prize," "Second Prize," etc. and each winner lands on their specific prize in a single drop.
